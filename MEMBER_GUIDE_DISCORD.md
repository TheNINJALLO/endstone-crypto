Welcome to the Crypto Market! This is your quick guide to earning and trading cryptocurrency on the server.

## Getting Started (3 Easy Steps)

### 1. Open the Market
Type in chat:
```

```
This opens the main menu with everything you need!

**BEST REWARDS (Highest Drop Chance):**
- Ancient Debris (3.2x multiplier) - RAREST, BEST REWARDS
- Deepslate Diamond Ore (2.8x multiplier)
- Emerald Ore (2.4x multiplier)
- Diamond Ore (2.0x multiplier)

**GREAT REWARDS:**
- Redstone Ore (1.2x multiplier)

**GOOD REWARDS:**
- Iron Ore (0.8x multiplier)

### 3. Check Your Earnings
Type `/crypto` and click "My Portfolio" to see:
- Your Money balance (shown on scoreboard)
- All the crypto coins you own
- Total value of your portfolio

## Basic Trading

**Buying Crypto:**
1. Type `/crypto`
2. Click "Buy Crypto"
3. Pick a coin
4. Enter how much you want
5. Click "Buy Now"

**Selling Crypto:**
1. Type `/crypto`
2. Click "Sell Crypto"
3. Pick a coin you own
4. Enter how much to sell
5. Click "Sell Now"

**Checking Prices:**
1. Type `/crypto`
2. Click "Market Prices"
3. Click any coin to see its price chart

Prices change automatically every 30 seconds!

---

## Trading with Other Players

**Create an Offer (Sell to a Player):**
1. Type `/crypto`
2. Click "P2P Trading"
3. Click "Create Offer"
4. Choose your coin
5. Set quantity and price
6. (Optional) Enter player name for private offer
7. Click "Create Offer"

**Accept Someone's Offer:**
1. Type `/crypto`
2. Click "P2P Trading"
3. Click "View All Offers"
4. Find the offer you want
5. Type: `/crypto accept <offer_id>`

**Cancel Your Offer:**
1. Type `/crypto`
2. Click "P2P Trading"
3. Click "My Offers"
4. Type: `/crypto cancel <offer_id>`

## Useful Commands

**Essential Commands:**
```
/crypto                    # Open main menu (USE THIS!)
/crypto holdings           # See what you own
/crypto coins              # List all coins & prices
/crypto price <symbol>     # Check a specific price
/crypto chart <symbol>     # See price chart
```

**Trading Commands:**
```
/crypto market buy <symbol> <qty>      # Buy from bank
/crypto market sell <symbol> <qty>     # Sell to bank
/crypto send <player> <symbol> <qty>   # Send coin to player
```

**Note:** If a player name has spaces, wrap it in quotes: `/crypto send "Player Name" NINJ 5`

**P2P Commands:**
```
/crypto offer <player> <symbol> <qty> <price>  # Create offer
/crypto offers                                  # See your offers
/crypto offers all                              # See all offers
/crypto accept <id>                             # Accept an offer
/crypto cancel <id>                             # Cancel your offer
```

**Note:** If a player name has spaces, wrap it in quotes: `/crypto offer "Player Name" NINJ 5 100`

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I get my first coins?**
A: Go mining! Breaking ores gives you free crypto.

**Q: What's the difference between Money and Credits?**
A: Money is shown on your scoreboard and is what you use for trading. Credits are an internal system (mostly for compatibility).

**Q: Can I lose money?**
A: Yes! If you buy at a high price and sell at a low price, you'll lose money. Watch the charts!

**Q: Are prices the same for everyone?**
A: Yes! All players see the same prices at the same time.

**Q: What happens to my coins if I log off?**
A: They're saved! You'll have them when you come back.

**Q: Can I give coins to friends?**
A: Yes! Use `/crypto send <player> <symbol> <qty>`

**Q: What are the fees?**
A: Each coin has different fees (usually 0.1% to 0.5%). The fee is shown when you trade.

**Q: How often do prices change?**
A: Prices update automatically every 30 seconds.

**Q: Can I see price history?**
A: Yes! Use `/crypto chart <symbol>` to see a price chart.

**Q: Which ores should I mine?**
A: Ancient Debris and Deepslate Diamond Ore give the best rewards! But all ores have a chance to drop crypto.

**Q: Does mining depth matter?**
A: YES! Mining below Y=16 gives you 2x better chances and bonus amounts. Go deep!

**Q: Which coins are best for beginners?**
A: Start with low-price coins like LUX, CARBN, RED, or FERRO. They're easier to trade and less risky.

**Q: Which coins are best for making profit?**
A: High-volatility coins like OVOID, BCON, AVIA, and NSTAR have bigger price swings, but they're riskier.
